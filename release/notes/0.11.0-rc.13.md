- Changed: a copy of the game built without a downloads address now builds and plays; it does not offer updates, and when the server no longer supports its version it says it cannot download or install updates.
- Changed: the game's code from GitHub (a clone or a ZIP) now holds everything Unity needs to build it; no script has to run first.
- Changed: a table card removed with Will of the West, or with any die and an Elven Ring (the Palantíri, The Lore of the Elven Rings), lights only for a Will of the West die or once you have clicked an Elven Ring; with another die it stays greyed and says how.
- New: in a battle, clicking a character's portrait opens that character's window (skills, Leadership and so on), as the Characters tab does.
- New: what a card draws at random is shown in its own window: Challenge of the King's three Hunt tiles turn up one by one, then the result; for Lure of the Ring (once the server is updated) a light runs over the Fellowship and stops on the Companion picked.
- New: the Shadow's Character die moves the Nazgûl straight from their army: unmark every unit in the region's panel and the Nazgûl fly alone, or click the new Move Nazgûl button in the army's section.
- Changed: Faramir's Rangers says when it recruits nothing because no Free Peoples Army stands in Osgiliath, before you play it and with its roll.
- Changed: while you choose where a Companion goes, the game says when a Shadow Stronghold on the way (such as Moria) stops him, and why a near region is out of reach.
- New: Settings has a Play section; with *Ask before ending with moves left* on (the default), the ✓ that would end an action while an army, a figure or a recruitment point is still unused asks first.
- Changed: the Mordor gate you may enter by is larger, pulses and is captioned *Enter Mordor* on the map; the first time in a game the Fellowship may enter, a window asks whether to enter now (Settings › Play can switch it off).
- New: Settings › Play › *Roll my own dice and draws* (on by default, as are the Play section's other two questions): your combat rolls, your cards' dice and draws and the Hunt tiles you draw by the rules wait for your click on a *Roll* / *Draw* button before their result shows, every Hunt waits for *Start the Hunt* for both players, and the Free Peoples see the Shadow's Hunt re-roll after *Re-roll the Hunt*; a re-roll after your own *Reroll selected* never waits.
- New: as the Shadow (or watching), each Free Peoples answer to the Hunt shows in a window beside the Hunt window: which character's ability, which card or the Ring, its text, and what it did (the damage taken off, the Companion who left, the Corruption).
- Changed: when a card has you choose figures in a region (Hill Trolls, Rage of the Dunlendings…), the ones you can still choose stay lit while more can be chosen; only those that cannot be chosen go dark.
