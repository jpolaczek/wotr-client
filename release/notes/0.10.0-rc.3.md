- New: ranked games. Find game pairs you with a player whose choices fit yours: the expansions, your side and the pace. Every move has a time limit, each player has three pauses, and a ranked game is played to its end.
- New: a rating for your account, shown after each ranked game, and Leaderboards with the top 25 players.
- New: if your connection drops during a ranked game, the game brings you back to the board as you left it, with the clock right.
- New: avatars. A small picture stands beside your name in chat, at the table and in the lists. Every account starts with the Eye or the Ring, and on your account page, which Settings opens, you can choose a soldier of any nation.
- New: Quit game on the sign-in screen.
- New: in the Hunt window, click a stage to see how it stood. The rest of the game stays as it is, and Esc or the marked stage returns.
