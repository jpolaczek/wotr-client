- New: the expansions Lords of Middle-earth (with the Council of Rivendell), Kings of Middle-earth and The Fate of Erebor, alone or together, with their characters, dice, Rings, Ruler tokens and cards.
- New: units and characters behave like tabletop miniatures: click one to lift it, or drag it onto a lit target; any other release puts it back.
- New: every character ability has its own icon. Click the icon to use the ability; its tooltip describes it, and an ability's variants stand together in one row.
- New: a character whose ability you can use now lights up wherever it stands: on the map, in the Characters panel and in the Fellowship panel.
- New: a character's window shows where the character stands as a picture of the place and always opens beside the Characters panel.
- Changed: every option with a picture on the board is chosen on the board itself. The lit object's tooltip says what the click does and which effect offers it, and a card's Finish is the console's ✓.
- Changed: everything a hint asks you to click glows clearly where it stands, including dice, decks, Rings, the Hunt Box and table cards.
- Changed: one marker per character; a character with several versions switches inside it.
- Changed: a standard corruption attempt draws all its tiles with the ruler's click; Sequential Corruption still draws them one by one.
- Fixed: both sides' rows stay level in the battle window, also after abilities such as Red Wrath.
- Fixed: the Effects panel keeps every source above the bottom bar, effect names fit their tiles, and a card's details open beside the panel.
