First installable release of War of the Ring Online (release candidate).

- Installers for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux x64. The Linux AppImage also runs on the Steam Deck in desktop mode or added to Steam as a non-Steam game; the Deck is experimental for now. Windows follows after its tests.
- The game keeps itself up to date: an optional update asks before downloading, a required one downloads in the background and installs after "Restart and update", and Settings → Repair installation reinstalls the current version. Your settings and local records stay.
- Accounts are created in the browser with an email address; a forgotten password is reset by email.
- Online 1v1 with the base game, Lords of Middle-earth, Kings of Middle-earth and Fate of Erebor.
