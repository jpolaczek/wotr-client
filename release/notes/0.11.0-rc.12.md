- Changed: in Settings, the sound section's main switch now reads "Play sounds" instead of repeating "Sound".
- Changed: a folded battle window now shows a Show battle button beside its step, and its unfold symbol is lit, so the whole window is easy to bring back.
- Fixed: your own move's animations (a nation's token on the Political Track, the Ring on the Fellowship's tracks, figures, cards and dice) no longer wait for the computer's quick answer to finish playing; they start at once, and the answer plays after them. A track that changed while its panel was closed no longer replays that old move when the panel is opened later.
- Fixed: a card whose extra attack rolls before the Combat roll (such as Charge) is shown once, at its own roll; its losses and the Combat roll follow without the card being shown a second time.
- Fixed: in the battle window, a Combat card keeps its size when only one or a few are in your hand, instead of stretching across the window.
- Fixed: after an Ents' card, when you may play another Character Event, the cards you cannot play are greyed even when none of them qualifies.
- Fixed: with a Character die chosen, clicking a companion and then clicking it again gives the die back, so the Fellowship's march can be clicked again.
- Fixed: an opponent's played card no longer flashes in the middle of the screen for a moment before it flies out of their hand.
