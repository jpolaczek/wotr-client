Customer typography uses two families on SIL Open Font License 1.1:

| Font | Authors / copyright | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cinzel SemiBold | Copyright 2020 The Cinzel Project Authors | [Full OFL](unity/Wotr.Client/Assets/StreamingAssets/ThirdPartyLicenses/cinzel-OFL.txt) |
| Alegreya Regular and SemiBold | Copyright 2011 The Alegreya Project Authors | [Full OFL](unity/Wotr.Client/Assets/StreamingAssets/ThirdPartyLicenses/alegreya-OFL.txt) |

Attached TTF are static scales of original projects. [Manifest](unity/Wotr.Client/Assets/Wotr/Resources/Wotr/Fonts/sources.json) save pinned sources, SHA-256 full licenses and author information go to build as readable text files in `StreamingAssets/ThirdPartyLicenses`.
