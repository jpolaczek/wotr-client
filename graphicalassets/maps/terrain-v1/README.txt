The site was created with an image_gen, without CLI/API and without being re-drawed by AI grid regions. build_map.py composes the existing document SVG: a settlement illustration under paths, preserves all 105 identifier, 323 border path, viewBox 335 40 1632 1338 and original coordinates. It does not interfere with the region-map files or customer rules or geometry. The PNG extension does not add information to the generated illustration; the actual size of the ground layer is stored in verification.json.

The first review detected a lake misread from the afterlife of North Downs, the scattered shores of the two internal seas, too large a marsh area, a forest at the site of the Shire fields and an excessive structure with a waterfall at Minas Tirith. refinement.prompt.txt Additional fit of the silhouettes of Evendim, Rhun and Nurnen and the shift of Minas Tirith and Barad-dûr describe alignment.prompt.txt and correction.prompt.txt The control of the region's mask showed that the full image continued to place the base of the white town in Lossarny. city-detail.prompt.txt describes the local enlargement editing, then folded in the original position of the fragment. Rebuild From the project directory, in environment with dependencies graphicalassets/region-map/tools/requirements.txt: python3 graphicalassets/maps/terrain-v1/build_map.py

The script checks the input, compatibility of the tracks and identifier, the lack of text elements SVG and the format PNG, then plays composition and preview. It does not re-create the image-generator model. The control of the subtitles inside the illustration and location of the objects requires an overview; the lack of elements SVG text itself is not proof of the lack of text in the raster.

Range of acceptance and limitations This is an illustrated map base for later styling. The paths of the regions are preserved accurately; the drawing of the site is not a measurement or a new geometry of motion. A slight fit of slopes, trees, hydrography and construction may require further artistic correction. The dimensions of the result file do not mean native illustration of 6K. The file verification does not replace the audit of rules or integration of Unity.
