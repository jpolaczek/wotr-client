Common natural elements adopted
- Blue Mountains, Mistlety, Grey, White, Erebor, Iron Hills and the border of Mordor; Ordruin as a
  small summit, without imaginary eruption and smoke.
- Old Forest, Mirkwood, Lorien, Fangorn, small Lindon forests and forest on the north-east side of
  Rhun. A range simplified to aggregates.
- Main rivers: western network, Anduina with main tributaries, Isen, Gondor River, Celduina/Carnen,
  Harnen; small Long Lake.
- Evendim, Rhun, Nurnen and the coast: their contour gives the current SVG, the interior of the
  water remains white. We do not generate another outline of the shores.
- Few hills in confirmed clusters of North Downs/Weather Hills and Emyn Muil and modest signpost
  Dead Marshes.

Unaccepted Buildings with MAP-02 have no common visual counterpart in all three maps: without drawn locks, towers, sediments and ports. Name or dot settlement is not the basis for designing its architecture. Ornamental ships, creatures, compass, cartouches, frame, texture of parchment, random stones, fields and filling of empty regions with hills. Local, uncertain details and divergent small clusters of trees remain empty.

It is a visual inventory of groups of objects, not measuring each tree or peak. The shapes of mountains and forests are to be simplified; the geometry of the game remains separate.
