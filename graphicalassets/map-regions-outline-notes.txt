Historical control of first versions:

PNG v3: 1492 × 1054, alpha channel; 603598 pixels completely transparent. The subtitles, sediment symbols, terrain illustrations, frame and track board were removed. In the third attempt, most of the double river lines replaced the single boundaries.

The boundaries are not an approved 1:1 mapping. The generator simplifies the shapes and leaves fine artifacts of the coast. Control of the combined white areas (RGB > 170, alpha > 200, area > 200 pixels) gave 109 areas in v3 and 123 in v2. The game catalog includes 105 regions. The raster area is not automatically a region of the game: islands, breaks and artifacts affect the result. No assignment of each field to the name of the region or control of all common borders and neighborhoods has been performed. Do not use this file as a source of traffic rules or a ready set of masks of regions without this verification.

The regions for separate animation should be created by dividing one verified geometry, with common edges and a fixed coordinate system. Separate regenerating of each region's shape can cause gaps and mismatches of elements.
